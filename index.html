Full-stack project

ResumeIQ

An AI-powered resume analyzer that turns a CV into interview prep and clear, fixable feedback.

Project overview

ResumeIQ reads an uploaded CV like a recruiter would. It predicts the interview questions a candidate is likely to face, flags weak or unprofessional writing, and helps the user prepare for a specific company and role.

Key features

  • Interview prep: likely questions based on the skills and projects on your CV, with what the interviewer is testing and how to answer
  • Company prep: pick a company, an optional role and a job description to get practice questions, a CV match score out of 100, matching and missing skills, likely interview rounds, a prep plan and the sources used
  • Honest question labels: each question is marked as reported by candidates (with a link to the page) or predicted
  • Language check: wrong or badly written keywords, unprofessional words, grammar, weak phrases, formatting and missing details, each with a suggested fix
  • CV scores: overall, ATS, clarity, professionalism and impact
  • One CV per user: re-uploading the same CV returns the saved result without a new AI call, and "Try another CV" replaces the old one to keep the database small
  • Accounts: register and login, Google sign-in, password reset by email, and PDF, DOCX or TXT uploads up to 5 MB

How to use

  1. Register, or sign in with Google.
  2. Upload your CV from the dashboard.
  3. Review your scores, interview questions and language fixes.
  4. Open Company prep, choose a company and role, and practise.
